# Tata iQ – GenAI Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation

A virtual job simulation completed through Forage, focused on using data analytics, predictive modeling, stakeholder communication, and responsible AI concepts to address credit card delinquency at Geldium Finance.

## 📌 Project Overview

The simulation focused on helping Geldium Finance understand and predict customer delinquency risk and translate analytical findings into responsible business recommendations.

The work covered four major tasks:

1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
2. Predictive Modeling Approach
3. Stakeholder Recommendation Report
4. AI-Powered Collections Strategy

## 📂 Tasks Completed

### Task 1 – Exploratory Data Analysis

Performed EDA on Geldium Finance's customer dataset to identify:

- Missing and inconsistent data
- Key customer characteristics
- Delinquency distribution
- Potential risk indicators
- Relationships between financial variables and delinquency

**Deliverable:** EDA Report

### Task 2 – Predictive Model Plan

Designed a conceptual predictive modeling approach for identifying customers at risk of delinquency.

**Model:** Logistic Regression

**Key input features:**
- Credit Utilization
- Debt-to-Income Ratio
- Missed Payments
- Credit Score
- Income

**Evaluation metrics:**
- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1 Score
- AUC-ROC
- Confusion Matrix
- Fairness checks

**Deliverable:** Predictive Model Plan

### Task 3 – Stakeholder Report

Translated analytical findings into business recommendations for Geldium's Head of Collections.

The report includes:

- Top delinquency risk factors
- High-risk customer segments
- SMART business recommendation
- Ethical and responsible AI considerations
- Explainability and fairness considerations

**Deliverable:** Stakeholder Report

### Task 4 – AI-Powered Collections System

Designed a high-level concept for an AI-powered collections system covering:

- Customer data inputs
- Decision logic
- Recommended actions
- Learning loop
- Agentic AI and human oversight
- Responsible AI guardrails
- Expected business and customer outcomes

**Deliverable:** PowerPoint Presentation
